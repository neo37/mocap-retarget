Photo → 3D pose

Drop in a photo of a person: MediaPipe finds 33 body landmarks and the page draws them as a figure you can spin. The image never leaves your machine — detection runs in WebAssembly right here. This is the same pose representation that mocap-retarget feeds into a rigged model, one frame of it.
